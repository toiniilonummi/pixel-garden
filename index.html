📲 INSTALL TO GET STARTED

Pixel Garden works best as an app on your Home Screen. Please install it first — it only takes 30 seconds!

1
TAP THE SHARE BUTTON At the bottom of your Safari browser, tap the share icon — it looks like a box with an arrow pointing up.
⬆️ Share
2
TAP "ADD TO HOME SCREEN" Scroll down in the menu until you find it.
➕ Add to Home Screen
3
TAP ADD Confirm by tapping Add in the top-right corner.
✅ Add
4
OPEN FROM YOUR HOME SCREEN 🌱 Find the Pixel Garden icon on your Home Screen and tap it to open the app and get started!
Your garden data is saved privately on your device and works offline.
